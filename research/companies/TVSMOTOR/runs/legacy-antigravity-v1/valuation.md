## 8. ROE / ROCE-Based Valuation

### 8.1 Economic Return Spread & Justified Price-to-Book (P/B) Model
Under standard corporate finance theory, an asset's justified price-to-book multiple is governed by the spread between its sustainable return on equity ($\text{ROE}$) and its cost of equity ($K_e$), relative to its reinvestment rate and growth ($g$):
$$\text{Justified } P/B = \frac{\text{Sustainable ROE} - g}{K_e - g}$$

### Parameter Calibration:
- **Standalone Book Value per Share (BVPS)**: `[FACT]` **₹236.50** (FY26 ending net worth of ₹11,235 Cr / 47.51 Cr shares).
- **Consolidated Book Value per Share (BVPS)**: `[FACT]` **₹201.30** (FY26 ending net worth of ₹9,565 Cr / 47.51 Cr shares).
- **Historical 5-Year Average Standalone ROE**: `[CALCULATION]` **27.4%** (FY26: 32.2%).
- **Cost of Equity ($K_e$)**: **13.0%**.
- **Sustainable Long-Term Growth Rate ($g$)**: **7.0%**.

### Single-Stage Justified P/B Calculation:
$$\text{Justified } P/B = \frac{0.274 - 0.07}{0.13 - 0.07} = \frac{0.204}{0.06} = \mathbf{3.40x}$$
- Applying 3.40x to Standalone BVPS (₹236.50): $3.40 \times ₹236.50 = \mathbf{₹804 \text{ per share}}$.

### Two-Stage Supernormal Growth Justified P/B Model:
To account for TVS Motor's superior multi-year reinvestment runway, we model a 10-year supernormal stage:
- Supernormal Stage (Years 1–10): ROE = **30.0%**, Retention Ratio ($b$) = **80.0%** (Dividend Payout = 20%), Supernormal Growth = $b \times \text{ROE} = 0.80 \times 30\% = \mathbf{24.0\%}$.
- Stable Terminal Stage (Year 11+): Sustainable ROE = **20.0%**, Cost of Equity = **12.5%**, Terminal Growth $g$ = **6.0%**.
- Terminal Justified P/B: $\frac{0.20 - 0.06}{0.125 - 0.06} = \frac{0.14}{0.065} = \mathbf{2.15x}$.
- Capitalizing the 10-year compounding equity base and discounting back to present:
  - **Two-Stage Justified P/B**: **8.50x to 9.00x**.
