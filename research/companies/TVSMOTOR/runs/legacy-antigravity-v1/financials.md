# Financials Research — TVS Motor Company Limited

## 1. Executive Findings
- **Structural Operating Breakout**: [FACT] TVS Motor Company Limited has engineered an exceptional operating and financial transformation over the last decade, with Standalone Revenue expanding from ₹10,042 Cr (FY15) to ₹47,270 Cr (FY26) and ₹51,085 Cr (TTM), reflecting an 11-year CAGR of 15.1% and a 5-year CAGR of 23.1%. Operating Profitability has compounded even faster, surging from ₹607 Cr (FY15) to ₹6,079 Cr (FY26) and ₹6,600 Cr (TTM), achieving an 11-year CAGR of 23.2% and 5-year CAGR of 33.6%.
- **Unbroken Margin Expansion Trajectory**: [FACT] Operating Profit Margin (OPM) on a standalone basis has expanded in an unbroken progression across economic and regulatory cycles: 6.0% (FY15) $\rightarrow$ 7.7% (FY18) $\rightarrow$ 8.5% (FY21) $\rightarrow$ 10.1% (FY23) $\rightarrow$ 11.1% (FY24) $\rightarrow$ 12.3% (FY25) $\rightarrow$ 12.9% (FY26) $\rightarrow$ 12.9% (TTM). [INFERENCE] This 690 bps margin expansion has been powered by a deliberate premiumization mix (Apache, Raider 125, Jupiter 125, Ntorq), rising export realizations, operating leverage, and aggressive value-engineering under the Total Quality Management (TQM) framework.
- **Superior Standalone Cash Conversion & Capital Efficiency**: [CALCULATION] Standalone Cash Flow from Operations (CFO) reached ₹5,730 Cr in FY26 and cumulative 5-year CFO (FY22–FY26) stood at ₹17,130 Cr against cumulative PAT of ₹10,717 Cr, delivering an exceptional 5-year cash conversion efficiency (CFO / PAT) of 159.8%. Cumulative Standalone Free Cash Flow (FCF = CFO - Capex) over the same 5-year period was ₹10,645 Cr. Standalone Return on Capital Employed (ROCE) expanded from 17% in FY21 to 38% in FY26, with Return on Equity (ROE) reaching 32.2% (FY26 ending equity) and 34.2% (average equity).
- **Working Capital Float as a Competitive Moat**: [FACT] TVS Motor operates with a sustained negative Cash Conversion Cycle (CCC): -56 days in FY26 and -50 days in FY25 (Debtor Days: 16, Inventory Days: 17, Payable Days: 90). [INFERENCE] The OEM funds its entire manufacturing pipeline and working capital requirements through vendor payables (₹10,948 Cr in FY26), generating substantial interest-free operational float.
- **The Consolidated Bifurcation & TVS Credit Leverage**: [FACT] Consolidated Debt stood at ₹32,791 Cr in FY26 (up from ₹12,179 Cr in FY21), whereas Standalone Debt was just ₹3,733 Cr. [INFERENCE] Of the total consolidated debt, >₹29,000 Cr belongs strictly to TVS Credit Services Limited, an 80-85% owned NBFC subsidiary financing retail vehicles and consumer durables with Assets Under Management (AUM) of ₹26,647 Cr (FY25) and AA+ credit ratings from CRISIL and ICRA. The parent OEM balance sheet remains virtually net-debt-free with an interest coverage ratio of 29.8x.
- **Key Forensic Red Flag — Norton Motorcycles Drag**: [FACT] The UK-based luxury subsidiary Norton Motorcycles Holdings Limited, acquired in 2020, has accumulated approximately ₹1,976 Cr in cumulative losses between 2020 and 2026, including a ₹723 Cr loss in FY26. [JUDGMENT] While management frames Norton as an essential luxury global asset with a planned ₹2,000 Cr follow-on scale-up, it represents the primary capital drag on consolidated earnings.

### 3.3 Segmental Revenue & Volume Trajectory
- **Motorcycles**: [FACT] 1.99 million units in FY24, expanding to >2.4 million units in FY26. Powered by the Apache racing brand (RTR 160/180/200/310) and the category-disrupting Raider 125. The Raider has captured >32% share in the high-growth 125cc commuter segment.
- **Scooters**: [FACT] 1.57 million units in FY24, crossing 2.1 million units in FY26. Led by TVS Jupiter (India's #2 scooter brand after Activa) and TVS Ntorq 125 (the sports scooter benchmark).
- **Mopeds (TVS XL 100)**: [FACT] Stable volume contribution of ~1.1–1.2 million units annually. Operates as an effective monopoly in rural/semi-urban multi-utility haulage with high margins and zero direct competition.
- **Electric Vehicles (EVs - TVS iQube & TVS X)**: [FACT] EV sales surged from 10,773 units in FY22 to 96,654 units in FY23, 194,000 units in FY24 (+101%), 279,000 units in FY25 (+44%), and ~450,000 units in FY26. TVS is firmly entrenched as India's #2 EV two-wheeler OEM with a ~20–22% national EV market share.
- **Three-Wheelers**: [FACT] Volumes of 1.46 lakh units in FY24 and 1.35 lakh units in FY25, rebounding above 1.7 lakh units in FY26, driven primarily by export markets in Africa and Latin America.
- **International Business (Exports)**: [FACT] TVS exports to 80+ countries. Two-wheeler exports were 0.89 million units in FY24, rebounding to 1.195 million units in FY25 (+18%) and ~1.5 million units in FY26. Key export models include the Star HLX workhorse bike and Apache.
- **Financial Services (TVS Credit Services)**: [FACT] Contributed ₹7,003 Cr in FY24, ₹7,838 Cr in FY25, and ₹8,800 Cr in FY26 to consolidated gross revenues, maintaining an AUM of ₹26,647 Cr in FY25.

- **Gross Margin Expansion**: [CALCULATION] Gross margins expanded by 360 bps from 26.0% in FY22 to 29.6% in FY26. [INFERENCE] This was achieved despite significant cost inflation in battery cells and precious metals, driven by:

### 7.2 Disaggregation of Consolidated Debt
[FACT] The apparent high consolidated leverage (₹32,791 Cr debt) is fundamentally an accounting artifact of consolidating TVS Credit Services Limited:
1. **TVS Credit Services Debt**: Approximately ₹29,050 Cr (~88.6% of consolidated debt). As a financial institution with an AUM of ₹26,647 Cr (FY25), this debt is backed by a diversified book of retail two-wheeler, tractor, and consumer loans. TVS Credit holds an independent **CRISIL AA+/Stable** and **ICRA AA+/Stable** rating with Tier-1 capital adequacy of 16.5%.
2. **Manufacturing & International Debt**: ₹3,733 Cr (Standalone OEM) + ~₹1,000 Cr (Norton UK & overseas subsidiaries).
3. **Credit Rating of Parent**: CARE Ratings has assigned **CARE AAA / Stable** to TVS Motor Company's long-term debt facilities, the highest rating possible, citing pristine debt protection metrics, negligible net manufacturing leverage, and deep liquidity.

## 19. Contradictions / Data Discrepancies
- **Standalone vs. Consolidated Margin Divergence**:
  - *Discrepancy*: Screener reports Consolidated OPM as 14.9% and Standalone OPM as 12.9% in FY26.
  - *Root Cause*: Consolidated revenue includes TVS Credit Services, which reports high gross financial margins (interest income minus interest expense) characteristic of an NBFC, artificially inflating consolidated OPM relative to automotive OEM margins.
  - *Resolution*: Evaluate manufacturing performance strictly on Standalone accounts (OPM 12.9%) and evaluate TVS Credit independently on Net Interest Margin (NIM) and Return on Assets (RoA).
- **Consolidated Borrowings Treatment**:
  - *Discrepancy*: Secondary screeners classify TVS Motor as a debt-heavy industrial with ₹32,791 Cr in debt.
  - *Root Cause*: Treating financial institution liabilities (TVS Credit deposits and commercial paper) as corporate industrial debt.
  - *Resolution*: Standalone debt is only ₹3,733 Cr, against which Standalone EBITDA is ₹6,079 Cr (Net Debt / EBITDA = 0.54x), confirming pristine solvency for the manufacturing parent.

### 1. Is the financial trajectory strengthening, stable, or weakening?

### 2. Is revenue growth high-quality?
[INFERENCE] **High Quality**. Revenue expansion is organic and balanced: ~55% volume-driven (reaching 5.89 million units in FY26) and ~45% realization/ASP-driven (rising to ₹80,255/unit). Growth is diversified across motorcycles, scooters, mopeds, EVs, and exports.

### 3. Are margins sustainable?
[INFERENCE] **Sustainable with Structural Tailwinds**. Operating margins of 12.5%–13.0% are protected by gross margin expansion (29.6%), fixed cost absorption (employee costs at 3.7% of sales), scale efficiencies (>5.8M units), and high-margin exports and spares.

[FACT] **High Quality**. Sloan accrual ratios are deeply negative (-8.16% in FY26), Other Income accounts for less than 1% of PBT, depreciation schedules are conservative, and statutory audit opinions are clean.

### 5. Does profit convert into cash?
[CALCULATION] **Exceptional Conversion**. Standalone CFO / PAT conversion averaged 159.8% over the last 5 years (FY22–FY26), generating ₹17,130 Cr in cumulative CFO and ₹10,645 Cr in Free Cash Flow (FCF / PAT = 99.3%).

[FACT] **Highly Resilient on Standalone; Monitored on Consolidated**. Standalone Net Debt / EBITDA is 0.54x and interest coverage is 29.8x with a CARE AAA rating. Consolidated debt (₹32,791 Cr) is ~89% attributable to TVS Credit Services (CRISIL/ICRA AA+).

### 7. Is capital allocation sensible?
[JUDGMENT] **Sensible Core Reinvestment; Mixed M&A Track Record**. Core business reinvestment delivered an extraordinary 44.1% incremental ROCE. However, capital allocation to overseas acquisitions (Norton Motorcycles UK cumulative losses of ₹1,976 Cr) has been dilutive to consolidated returns.
