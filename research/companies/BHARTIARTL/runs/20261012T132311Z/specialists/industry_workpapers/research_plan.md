2. Profit pools: spectrum (state), towers (Indus), network vendors, operators, content/OTT, handset OEMs.
3. Reachable market: India telecom AGR/GR pool (TRAI), wireless subscriber base, penetration; Africa 14 markets.
4. Share over 10–15 years: TRAI wireless subscriber share and AGR (revenue) share FY2010–FY2026; Airtel vs Jio vs Vi vs BSNL.
5. Cycle: 2010–2016 hyper-competition (12 players) → 2016 Jio entry/price war → consolidation (2017–2019) → AGR shock (2019) → tariff repairs Dec-2019, Nov-2021, Jul-2024 (+ any 2025–2026) → 5G capex peak FY2023–FY2025.
6. Macro: INR/USD (capex, Africa), naira/other African FX, rates, inflation, GDP/consumption; spectrum prices.
7. Change vectors: Telecom Act 2023, satcom (Starlink/Kuiper/OneWeb), AGR relief for Vi, Jio IPO, FWA, 5G monetisation, OTT/AI.
