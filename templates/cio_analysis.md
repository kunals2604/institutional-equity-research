# CIO Independent Analysis — {company} ({ticker}) · run {run_id}

The CIO's own work beyond the nine specialists (`.claude/agents/cio.md` Stage 3 step 7). Every number comes from code in `calc/cio_*` and is a CIO-C ledger claim.

## 1. Primary reading by the CIO
Latest annual report (chairman's/MD letter, MD&A) and the Q&A of the latest two earnings calls: what changed the CIO's view, with locators.

## 2. Independent valuation cross-check
Method (different from valuation's primary method), inputs, output, and whether it confirms or contradicts the specialist range, and why.

## 3. Peer and consensus cross-check
Closest peers on growth, returns, leverage and multiples; consensus (ESTIMATE) where retrievable.

## 4. Quantitative score guides
`scripts/score_guides.py` output (BQ1, BQ4, BQ5 guide bands) and the WACC used.

## 5. What the specialists missed or under-weighted
At least three items, each with evidence and its effect on the conclusion.
